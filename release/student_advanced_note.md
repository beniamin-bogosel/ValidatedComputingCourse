# Further study after the introductory course

The core course deliberately starts with concrete interval computations and scalar validation before introducing small systems. Further study can include rigorous integration and ODEs, periodic orbits, invariant manifolds, Taylor models, and more general Newton–Kantorovich or radii-polynomial methods.

These topics require additional mathematics and implementation work. The course does not distribute or require the local advanced Julia course snapshot reviewed during planning.

For a mathematical continuation, consult Tucker's later chapters and Appendix A.4. Rump's [Verification methods survey](https://www.tuhh.de/ti3/rump/intlab/ActaNumerica2010.pdf) supplies broader instructor-level background. Use the [course reading map](../references.md) to connect these readings with the methods already studied.
