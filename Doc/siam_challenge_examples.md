# SIAM 100-Digit Challenge — quick reference

Folkmar Bornemann, Dirk Laurie, Stan Wagon, and Jörg Waldvogel,
*The SIAM 100-Digit Challenge: A Study in High-Accuracy Numerical Computing*.
The PDF is available in this directory.

Use as an optional source of examples for the validated computing course.
Consult individual chapters when developing the corresponding notebooks;
no full-book review or problem development is needed now.
Page numbers below are the printed book pages.

**First places to revisit:** Chapter 4 for interval arithmetic, subdivision,
interval Newton, and global optimization; Appendix D for alternative examples.
Chapters 2 and 7 may also supply geometry and linear-algebra applications.
Select or simplify problems to match introductory prerequisites.

Chapter 2 is a user-selected project candidate; see the brief
[mirror-trajectory project note](mirror_trajectory_project.md), including the
related laser/butterfly-effect video.

The original challenge requested ten digits for each of ten problems.
For our course, distinguish high-accuracy answers from rigorously certified
results: adapted assignments should state the claim and the required enclosure
or verification conditions explicitly.
